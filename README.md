# E-Commerce Customer Lifecycle & Revenue Segmentation Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary & Business Context
An international online retail business operating across multiple markets lacks visibility into customer purchasing behaviors, churn dynamics and revenue concentration. Currently, the commercial team applies a uniform, "one-size-fits-all" marketing and retention strategy across its entire customer base.

### The Problem
* **Margin Erosion:** Broad-spectrum promotional discounts are distributed uniformly, degrading gross margins on loyal accounts willing to purchase at full price.
* **Silent Churn:** High-lifetime-value customers discontinue purchasing without detection or proactive retention intervention.
* **Capital Inefficiency:** Marketing expenditures and customer service bandwidth are deployed evenly across accounts rather than concentrated where return on investment (ROI) is maximized.

### Project Objective
Transform over one million raw, uncurated transactional records into a dynamic **Recency, Frequency, Monetary (RFM)** customer segmentation framework. The deliverable is an actionable commercial lifecycle strategy that identifies core revenue drivers, mitigates customer churn, and optimizes targeted marketing ROI.
